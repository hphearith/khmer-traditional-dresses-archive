## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is Apsara Mode Tailor's shopfront seen from the street: a painted bilingual sign board, a striped awning, and a row of lit glass showcases with one dress in each. It refuses the museum collection page (cream ground, serif headline beside a hero photo, a grid of matted cards).

OWN-WORLD: Enamel sign blue owns the whole top band; sign yellow carries the painted Khmer lettering; signal red runs as pinstripe, awning stripe and the about panel; a cool tile white is the wall. Components are painted plaques (2px corners, inner pinstripe) and blue-framed glass cabinets with a lamp strip. Latin display: Big Shoulders, condensed sign-writer caps. Khmer: Noto Serif Khmer bold, always above and larger than the English on the sign.

STORY: The visitor knows at once this is a real Phnom Penh tailor's dresses, believes it because every dress is photographed in that shop, and walks the window: filter, search, open a dress, or step into the Community.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed blue sign: archive brand and account links painted along its top, the Khmer name in yellow at display scale with the English name under it, the description below. The red-and-white scalloped awning hangs under it with the two section tabs as plaques on its edge. Then a compact counter (heading, count, filters, search) and the showcase cabinets starting above the fold.

Signature interaction: the showcase lamps switch on cabinet by cabinet on first load, and a cabinet's lamp glows when it is pointed at or focused; on phones the showcase becomes a swipeable row of cabinets.
