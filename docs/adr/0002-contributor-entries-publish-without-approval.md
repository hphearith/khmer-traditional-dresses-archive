# Contributor entries publish without approval; the Curator moderates afterwards

A Contributor's entry goes public the moment they publish it. There is no pending-approval state. The Curator (a flag on an account) can afterwards hide or delete any Contributor entry. This departs from the course skeleton's wording "submit-review-publish": the "review" is the Contributor previewing their own Draft, plus the Curator's after-the-fact takedown. Submit-review-publish is a Sprint 3 feature. Status stays `proposed` until this reading is checked against the Sprint 3 brief.

## Considered Options

- **Curator approves every entry before it is visible.** Rejected: it makes the Curator a bottleneck for an archive meant to take open contributions, and was explicitly declined by the owner.
- **Publish immediately with no Curator powers.** Rejected: open signup plus public text and photos needs a takedown path.

## Consequences

- Entries have three states: Draft, Published, Hidden. Deleting is permanent and removes the entry's photo.
- Open signup plus instant publishing means spam or inappropriate content is visible until the Curator notices. Email-confirmed signup is the only upstream barrier.
